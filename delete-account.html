Account and data

Delete your HailOwner account

You can delete your account and everything in it at any time. It is permanent and cannot be undone.

In the app (fastest)

Open HailOwner → Settings → Delete my account, read what is removed, and confirm. Everything is deleted at once.

By e-mail (if you no longer have the app)

Write to us from any address and say you want your account deleted. If you can, include the support code shown in the app under Settings → Contact support, so we can find it. We delete the account and reply when it is done, within 30 days.

keyvan198954@gmail.com

What is deleted

  • Your vehicles and their reminders
  • Your alert history and your replies
  • Your emergency information and your close people
  • The link between your phone and your account (push notifications stop)
  • Your HailOwner cards stop working for good; the plastic card cannot be used again

If you bought a card on this website

The app account and a website order are separate. Include your order reference (HO-…) in your e-mail and we also delete the name and contact you gave when ordering. We may keep payment and tax records the law requires us to keep.
